Gulab Jamun

Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • Milk
  • Ghee
  • Baking Powder
  • Milk Powder
  • All-Purpose Flour (Maida)

Recipe

For Jamun (Dumpling)

  1. 1 cup (100g) Milk Powder (full-fat recommended)
  2. 3 tbsp All-Purpose Flour (Maida)
  3. 1 tbsp Ghee (clarified butter), melted
  4. ½ tsp Baking Powder
  5. 4-5 tbsp Warm Milk
  6. Oil or Ghee for deep frying

For Chasni (Sugar Syrup)

  1. 1½ cups Sugar
  2. 1½ cups Water
  3. 3-4 Green Cardamoms
  4. 1 tsp Lemon Juice
  5. 1 tsp Rose/Kewra Water (optional)
